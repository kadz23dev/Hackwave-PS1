Frequently Asked Questions

How does KaizenAI differ from naive AI testing tools? ▼
Naive AI testers take screenshots or read DOM text and ask an LLM if the test passed. When a checkout 500 error shows "Order confirmed" on screen, naive AI passes the test! KaizenAI enforces Guardrail G5: no LLM ever produces a verdict. Verdicts come strictly from backend state queries (e.g. database orders count) and network status intercepts. Steps may heal; outcomes may not.
Does KaizenAI write code directly to our web application source repository? ▼
No. Per decision settled in PRD §4 and §9, KaizenAI only repairs the test automation step plans. It never modifies your target application files. Your developers have full control over their code.
How does the Zero-LLM Step Replay Cache work? ▼
When a repair is proposed, validated in a fresh browser context, and approved, its step plan is stored with the start page content hash. On subsequent runs with the same page hash, KaizenAI reuses the plan with 0 LLM calls, executing at native Chromium Playwright speed.
Can our company manage multiple applications and environments? ▼
Yes! Your company portal supports unlimited sites, environments (staging, production, preview PRs), and test suites, completely isolated to your organization.
